# Message moves

Every message in `correspondence` is built from fixed moves. The structure is the coffee-chat method as practiced and written down by au5m. It works because it is simple and consistent, not because each note is clever. Do not add moves. Do not remove moves. Do not decorate.

Names in the examples below are placeholders. Replace them from the conversation.

## Outreach: who, why, ask, times

The user starts the conversation. A cold note to a hiring manager, a team lead, a recruiter, or a contact at a target company. Also a request for an intro or a referral.

```
Subject: [One line: who you are] <> [Company] Chat
Attachment: [profile.attachment_name]

Hi Jordan,

I hope you are doing well. [One sentence: who you are, from profile.one_line_bio.] [One sentence: why this company or team, naming something real.]

If your schedule permits, I would welcome a chance to learn more about [the role / your team / your experience at Company]. Would you be open to a call during any of these?

[Date]: [window] [time zone]
[Date]: [window] [time zone]
[Date]: [window] [time zone]

If not, happy to find another time that works for you.

[profile.sign_off]
[profile.first_name]
```

The bio is one sentence, in the user's own words. The "why" names something true about the company that the user can speak to if asked. If the user has not given times, leave them as marked blanks. Never invent availability.

## Follow-up: thanks, recap, ask, personal line

The user writes after a conversation. An interview, a panel, a coffee chat, an intro call, or a favor. Thank-you notes are follow-ups.

```
Subject: [Role or topic] follow-up

Hi Jordan,

Quick note to say thanks for your time [yesterday / on Friday]. Appreciated you walking me through [what they covered], specifically [one or two things they said].

[The ask: meet another person on the team, the next stage, or an intro.]

Hope [something personal from the conversation]!

[profile.sign_off]
[profile.first_name]
```

The recap is what they said, in their words where possible. It is not what the user said about themselves. If the user has not given a specific, ask one question: "What is one thing they said that you can point back to?" Do not draft around a vague recap.

The ask is the method's "who else should I talk to." The role pack's Ask patterns section gives the natural version for the user's field.

The personal line is one sentence with an exclamation mark: a weekend, a trip they mentioned, a shared detail. If there is nothing real, drop the line. Do not invent one.

## Reply: answer, then ask or times

Someone wrote to the user first. A recruiter message, an email, a scheduling request, a decline.

```
Hi Jordan,

Thanks for [the specific thing they sent: the job description, the context, the heads-up]. [Direct answer in one or two sentences.]

[Times if asked, as a list under the body.]

[profile.sign_off]
[profile.first_name]
```

Open on what they asked. Answer it. Then the ask or the times. A three-line message gets three lines back.

Decline:

```
Hi Jordan,

Thanks for sending this over. This one is not the right fit for what I am focused on, but I would be glad to hear from you if a [target title from profile] role opens up.

[profile.sign_off]
[profile.first_name]
```

## Nudge: reference, restate, times

The user sent a message and got no answer.

```
Hi Jordan,

Following up on my note from [day]. [One sentence restating the ask.] Any of these work?

[Date]: [window] [time zone]
[Date]: [window] [time zone]

[profile.sign_off]
[profile.first_name]
```

No apology for following up. No "I know you are busy." Wait two or three days before the first nudge. Two or three nudges is normal. Silence is not a no.

## Cover letter: hook on them, bridge, two proofs, ask

A cover letter is addressed to a person, so it is correspondence mode, not documents mode. It is short: four paragraphs, under 250 words, on one page.

```
Hi Jordan,

[One or two sentences on the specific thing about this role or company that interests you. Name it. This is the "react to what they said" move, applied to the posting.]

[One or two sentences: who you are, from profile.one_line_bio, and the bridge to this role.]

[Two proofs. Each one sentence, each from the resume, each with its outcome. Pick the two that match the posting's center of gravity.]

[The ask: a conversation. One sentence. Then a warm close.]

[profile.sign_off]
[profile.full_name]
```

Facts come only from the resume and `profile.md`. The two proofs are resume bullets rewritten as sentences, not new claims. Run the roster pass if the letter names any employer.

## Rules of engagement

- Send a follow-up within 24 hours of the conversation.
- Keep it short. The template sets the length. Do not pad and do not cut below the template.
- Always ask for a next step.
- Attach the resume on outreach. Name the attachment in the subject block.
- Availability goes as dates and windows in a list under the body, in the user's time zone from `profile.md`, chronological, two to three windows of two to four hours each.
- Send between 10am and 5pm in the recipient's time zone.
- Check the name and the company twice. One wrong name ends the thread.
- Once a time is confirmed, the user sends the calendar invite the same day.
- Greet by first name. Full sentences.
- "I hope you are doing well" is fine in outreach. "Thanks for taking the time" is fine only when the next clause names what the time covered.
